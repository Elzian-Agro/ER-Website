Blogs

LifeForce introduces a unique, science-backed contract ecosystem where trees are not just symbols of nature but economic units in a regenerative model. This blog post explores how LifeForce contracts manage tree units, allocate payments, and generate traceable oxygen-based environmental credits.

Escrow and Tree Guardian Model

Each LifeForce contract allocates 80% of funds to the tree guardian, 10% for monitoring (MRV), and 10% for admin/data. Enrolled farmers or landowners maintain the tree in healthy conditions for 4 years, monitored by measurable leaf mass growth.

Environmental Credits & Ownership

Annual payments are based on photosynthetic biomass. Environmental outputs like oxygen and clean water are recorded and transferred to investor ledgers. These credits can be sold or transferred via QR codes on verified markets.
