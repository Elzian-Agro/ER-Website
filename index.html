About

The nature of our business :
CPES ‘positive externalities’ deposited to the Global Commons

There is no ‘production technology’ that is more efficient than trees and plants to produce primary ecosystem services sucha as Oxygen. But the amount of PES that they provide varies greatly, depending on age, species, and the environment they grow in. While Oceanic Phototrophs produce more 'net Oxygen, terrestrial systems contribute an additional primary ecosystem service , the function of water purification through transpiration - Dr. Ranil Senanayake.

Every tree as it grows eventually reaches a ‘compensation point’, where the accumulation of Photosynthetic Biomass tapers off, net primary productivity ceases, and the tree has enough turn over to respire for maintaining its mature state. EarthRestoration’s methodological approach to validate the first 4 years of growth guarantees that the LifeForce validates net primary productivity.

Why we do it

CPES & the foundations of ‘onsetting’ Oxygen as a tangible economic opportunity

The Oxygen that is present in the air that we breathe Is the birth right of every organism that lives on this planet. It is free for everyone. However, the action of some to take out more than their share, without replacement, has created a condition where the Global Commons of air is being rapidly degraded. Free Molecular Oxygen (FMO) is one of the rarest substances in the known universe, it is found only in the atmosphere of planet earth. The only known The process that produces FMO biologically is photosynthesis.

How we do it

LifeForce approach for Contracted Primary Ecosystem Services (CPES)

1. A tree unit will be contracted to the landowner of each approved LifeForce community development project site. Each enrolled landowner or farmer has agreed to maintain the tree unit in good health and in accordance with the LifeForce Tree Farming Contract — signified by the leaf mass accumulation on the tree over the contracted time (4 years).

Tree species we Monitor, Record and Validate (MRV) using LifeForce : View MRV Tree Species List

2. Each LifeForce contract has an escrow account: 80% in favour of the tree guardian, 10% for monitoring, and 10% for admin and data management.

3. The tree guardian payment is done once a year for 4 years and is based on the weight of photosynthetic biomass (leaf mass) grown and maintained

4. At each annual MRV the PB accumulation = Oxygen and clean water generated will be ceded to the investors LifeForce ledger

How it works

What we do in terms of servicing each LifeForce unit is identified through Climate smart agriculture and Analog Forestry intervention modules that are accessible to the community of LifeForce landowners . The thematic areas of education for year 1 to 4 are identified.

1 YEAR

Thematic knowledge & Practice 1 : Soil management and farmland activities schedule

  • Soil bunds terraces.
  • Economic estimation of LifeForce contract.
  • Organic Manure compost : In every managed farmland.
  • Mulching : Bi-annual intermonsoon practice identified on a farming ‘activities calendar.
  • BioChar : Integrate value chains that collect and pelletize.

2 YEAR

Thematic knowledge & Practice 2 : Water Management

  • Identify water flow patterns in the farmed area.
  • Economic estimation of LifeForce contract.
  • Establish percolation pits and pond habitats analogous to natural habitats found in the region.

3 YEAR

Thematic knowledge & Practice 3 : Agroforestry / Perennial Crop Production

  • Economic estimation of LifeForce contract.
  • Boundary trees and hedgerows : native trees and shrubs / multi-purpose N-Fixing trees / EarthRestoration tree farming manual.
  • Food Forest / orchard design .
  • Reduce post harvest losses value add fruits and vegetables : Food preserving manual for annual crops in the tropics : Food and Drug Admin approved.

4 YEAR

Additional benefits can be scaled through income affording the database of homegarden farmers

  • Clean energy : Solar pumps.
  • Weather forecasting tools : Elzian AgroTec partners.
