Our Products

LifeForce Tokenized Primary Ecosystem Services

LifeForce tokens are value set to existing markets based on the volume of contractually produced Primary Ecosystem Services (atmospheric elements). They represent traceable, measurable ecosystem value with economic recognition.

1. Escrow Distribution

Each LifeForce contract allocates 80% to tree guardians, 10% to monitoring, and 10% to admin/data management.

2. Community Project Enrollment

Each approved project assigns a tree unit to a guardian farmer who maintains it per contract, measured by leaf mass growth.

3. Guardian Payouts

Annual payments over 4 years are based on the biomass (leaf mass) maintained by the tree guardian.

4. Ledger Integration

Each payout updates the LifeForce ledger with Oxygen and clean water produced, transferred to the investor’s balance.

5. Global Commons Deposit

Tokens represent deposited ecosystem services in the Global Commons, maintained in the owner’s ledger.

6. Immutable MRV Record

At the end of 4 years, an immutable data block records MRV details for permanent proof of service.

7. Market Transfer

Ownership is certified with a QR code and can be transferred or sold on select markets.
