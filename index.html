Our Products

LifeForce Tokenized Primary Ecosystem Services

LifeForce tokens are value set to existing markets based on the volume of contractually produced Primary Ecosystem Services (atmospheric elements). They represent traceable, measurable ecosystem value with economic recognition.

1. LifeForce Contract Escrow Allocation

Each LifeForce contract has an escrow account 80% in favour of the tree guardian, 10 % for monitoring and 10% to admin and data management.

2. Tree Guardian Assignment and Maintenance Commitment

A Unit (tree) will be given to the tree guardian of each approved LifeForce community development project design. Each enrolled farmer has agreed to maintain the plant in good health and in accordance to the LifeForce tree farming Contract --- signified by the leaf mass accumulation on the tree over time.

3. Annual Payment Based on Leaf Mass

The tree guardian payment is done once a year for 4 years and is based on the weight of photosynthetic biomass (leaf mass) maintained.

4. Environmental Credits Assigned at Payouts

At every payout, the Oxygen and clean water generated by the photosynthetic biomass will be ceded to the investors LifeForce ledger.

5. Global Commons Deposit via Ledger

This production will be deposited in the Global Commons by the owner who through his or her LifeForce ledger ‘transaction certificate’ will maintain his/her personal stocks as a deposit into the Global Commons.

6. Ledger Capped with Final MRV Data Block

At the end of the four year contract term the ledger is capped and an immutable DATA block of the LifeForce MRV record is created.

7. Ownership Record Transferable via QR Code

This record of ownership issued as a QR code could be sold or transferred on select markets.
