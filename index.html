Our Products

LifeForce Tokenized Primary Ecosystem Services

Each LifeForce token is Value Set to existing markets based on the volume of contractually produced Primary Ecosystem Services (atmospheric elements). LifeForce tokens provide a traceable ‘onset’ of primary ecosystem services backed by the new economic prospect of quantified ‘positive externalities’.

1. LifeForce Contract Escrow Allocation

Each LifeForce contract has an escrow account 80% in favour of the tree guardian, 10 % for monitoring and 10% to admin and data management.

2. Tree Guardian Assignment and Maintenance Commitment

A Unit (tree) will be given to the tree guardian of each approved LifeForce community development project design. Each enrolled farmer has agreed to maintain the plant in good health and in accordance to the LifeForce tree farming Contract --- signified by the leaf mass accumulation on the tree over time.

3. Annual Payment Based on Leaf Mass

The tree guardian payment is done once a year for 4 years and is based on the weight of photosynthetic biomass (leaf mass) maintained.

4. Environmental Credits Assigned at Payouts

At every payout, the Oxygen and clean water generated by the photosynthetic biomass will be ceded to the investors LifeForce ledger.

5. Global Commons Deposit via Ledger

This production will be deposited in the Global Commons by the owner who through his or her LifeForce ledger ‘transaction certificate’ will maintain his/her personal stocks as a deposit into the Global Commons.

6. Ledger Capped with Final MRV Data Block

At the end of the four year contract term the ledger is capped and an immutable DATA block of the LifeForce MRV record is created.

7. Ownership Record Transferable via QR Code

This record of ownership issued as a QR code could be sold or transferred on select markets.

Realizing value for
Photosynthetic Biomass

Primary Ecosystem Services (PES) are produced by the action of photosynthesis in Photosynthetic Biomass (leaf). These services are, Oxygen generation, Carbon Dioxide sequestration, Carbohydrate creation, groundwater cleansing and ambient cooling. Recent studies on this aspect of vegetation suggests a value of between USD 250-450 per acre per year for mature, diverse forest.

Explore the Oxygen Economy Watch Our Story

The Oxygen Economy is a framework based on EarthRestorations vision that assigns economic value to the Oxygen that is produced by privately contracted ecosystems.

The Oxygen Economy is a principled framework that assigns economic value to the oxygen that is produced by privately contracted ecosystems. While the idea might seem unusual at first glance, it stems from a growing recognition that oxygen is not just a “free” resource but a service provided by the earth’s ecosystems—particularly trees, plants, and other photosynthetic organisms. These ecosystems convert carbon dioxide into oxygen through the process of photosynthesis, supporting life on earth and maintaining the balance of our atmosphere. At its core, the Oxygen Economy aims to ensure that those who contract and monitor oxygen-producing ecosystems—such as farmlands, indigenous communities, and other rare habitats that demonstrate the adaptability and diversity of our planet —are fairly compensated for their efforts. It also holds industries and private-sector entities that benefit from oxygen consumption accountable for contributing to the sustainability of the resource.
